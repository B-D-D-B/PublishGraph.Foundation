About SDA: Who We Are & Where We're Going

Technology

At SDA, technology drives both artistic expression and accessibility. Our SDA-Frame system delivers dynamic, AI-assisted digital art feeds for artists, enthusiasts and businesses, seamlessly integrating real-time content into luxury spaces, corporate environments, and hospitality venues. Meanwhile, SDA Fusion, our proprietary artist tool, empowers creators to streamline AI-driven workflows, optimize artwork for commercial use, and simplify publishing across multiple platforms.

History

SDA’s roots trace back to the early ‘90s underground ANSI art scene, where digital artists thrived in the emerging world of pre-internet bulletin boards. What began as a pirate BBS art group has evolved into a decentralized AI artist collective, bridging classic digital artistry with cutting-edge AI innovation. This relaunch continues SDA’s legacy of experimentation, collaboration, and boundary-pushing creativity.
