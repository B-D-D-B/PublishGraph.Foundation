SDA Operators:

Infrastructure, Development

& Web3 Integration

SDA Operators are the backbone of SDA’s technical ecosystem, ensuring seamless platform functionality, development, and innovation. From maintaining core infrastructure to building Web3 integrations and supporting SDA-Frame cross-platform deployment, operators play a critical role in enabling the Collective’s technology-driven vision.

What Do SDA

Operators Do?

Operators manage and develop the technical framework that powers SDA, supporting everything from platform stability to next-generation Web3 integrations.

  • Infrastructure & Platform Maintenance – Oversee the health and stability of SDA’s digital ecosystem, including Discord servers, website operations, and content management systems.
  • SDA Review Magazine Platform – Design and implement the technical foundation for SDA Review, ensuring a seamless, interactive, and scalable content release system.
  • UI Development for SDA Fusion – Use front-end development skills to build SDA Fusion’s interface, integrating it with SDA’s partner low-code platform to enhance artist workflows.
  • Web3 & Blockchain Development – Build and maintain blockchain-based features that support SDA’s Authenticated Digital Collectibles (ADCs), including minting tools, smart contract integrations, and on-chain metadata management.
  • SDA-Frame Cross-Platform Deployment – Develop solutions that enable SDA-Frame’s digital art feeds to function across multiple hardware and software environments, optimizing for performance and compatibility.

How Operators

Are Supported

Operators at SDA are given access to cutting-edge tools, development environments, and collaborative resources to drive innovation and efficiency.

  • Access to SDA’s Internal Development Tools – Work with SDA’s evolving tech stack, from Web3 integrations to front-end development.
  • Collaboration with SDA Members – Join strategic discussions on platform growth, development priorities, and new feature rollouts.
  • Early Access to New Technologies – Gain hands-on experience with emerging blockchain, AI, and digital art display technologies.
  • Opportunities for Expanded Roles – As SDA scales, operators can take on new exciting technical directions, Web3 strategy, and platform engineering. We decide what we create.

How Operator

Earnings Work

Operators participate in SDA’s 5% revenue share model, earning passive income across all SDA revenue streams. As SDA expands, operators benefit from the success of the platform and its technology-driven services.

  • Authenticated Digital Collectibles (ADCs) Sales – A percentage of every ADC sale contributes to the operator revenue pool.
  • Commissions & Custom Artwork – A portion of SDA’s commission-based work is allocated to operators for maintaining and developing platform features.
  • SDA-Frame Premium Subscriptions – Revenue from businesses subscribing to SDA’s curated digital art feeds contributes to the pool.
  • Gallery & Licensing Deals – Any revenue generated from SDA’s curated gallery placements and brand licensing supports operator earnings.

As SDA’s technology infrastructure grows, operator earnings scale alongside it, making this a long-term, opportunity-driven role for technical specialists.

Why Join as

an SDA Operator?

Joining SDA as an Operator means becoming a key part of the technical backbone that powers our digital art ecosystem. Whether you're building Web3 integrations, developing SDA-Frame’s cross-platform deployment, or designing intuitive UI for artist tools, you’ll be shaping the future of digital art infrastructure while earning from SDA’s long-term growth.

  • Earn passive income from SDA’s 5% revenue pool, including ADC sales, commissions, and SDA-Frame subscriptions.
  • Play a critical role in SDA’s technical foundation, from Web3 integrations to front-end development and platform scaling.
  • Work with emerging technologies in blockchain, digital art display systems, and AI-driven creative tools.
  • Collaborate with artists, curators, and business teams to build seamless digital experiences.
  • Operate on your own schedule while contributing to a fast-growing, innovation-driven collective.
  • No conventional corporate oversight, we build what makes sense as a community to push the collective forward and energize digital content consumption.

Who Should Apply?

SDA is looking for skilled developers, blockchain engineers, and technical specialists eager to contribute to a rapidly evolving digital art ecosystem.

  • Web3 Developers & Blockchain Engineers – Those skilled in smart contract development, NFT integration, and decentralized applications.
  • Front-End Developers & UI/UX Designers – Developers with experience in modern JavaScript frameworks, interactive UI design, and platform integration.
  • Infrastructure & DevOps Specialists – Individuals experienced in maintaining digital platforms, managing cloud-based environments, and ensuring system scalability.
  • Digital Art & AI Technologists – Those interested in AI-assisted art tools, content automation, and media-driven application development.
  • SDA Members with or without Technical Expertise – Existing SDA contributors looking to take on a hands-on technical role within the collective.

SDA is currently seeking a limited number of operators to develop and maintain the technical infrastructure that powers our ecosystem. From managing Discord and website operations to building Web3 integrations, operators ensure that SDA’s platforms run smoothly and scale effectively.

 

If you're interested in shaping the future of SDA’s digital infrastructure and contributing to cutting-edge development projects, please contact us or join us on Discord to learn more.
