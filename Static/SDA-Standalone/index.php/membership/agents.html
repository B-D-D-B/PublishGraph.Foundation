SDA Agent Opportunities

SDA Agent collective members play a key role in expanding the reach of AI-driven digital art, forging partnerships that bring SDA-Frame and authenticated digital collectables into hotels, corporate environments, and luxury spaces. By integrating SDA’s technology and artwork into B2B partnerships and commercial settings, agents help shape the future of digital art experiences.

What Do SDA

Agents Do?

SDA Agents drive the adoption of SDA-Frame’s freemium digital art platform and secure B2B partnerships that bring SDA artwork into high-end locations. They focus on creating long-term partnerships that introduce SDA’s AI-assisted, blockchain-backed art solutions to businesses and institutions.

 

Key Responsibilities

  • Introduce SDA-Frame’s Free Customizable Feeds to businesses, encouraging seamless adoption and organic upgrades
  • Facilitate premium feed subscriptions, bespoke commissions, and NFT-backed art solutions for high-end clientele
  • Develop branded digital art integrations for immersive luxury experiences
  • Establish licensing and reseller agreements to expand SDA’s commercial reach

SDA Agents do not work on one-time sales commissions—they contribute to SDA’s expansion and earn from the success of the entire business.

Opportunities

Beyond Creation

Participation in SDA extends beyond creating art. The collective is structured to ensure that members who contribute—whether through art, promotion, curation, or technical development—share in its success. Revenue is distributed across artists and support roles, offering multiple pathways to benefit from involvement.

(Scroll down to learn more about revenue distribution.)

How Agent

Earnings Work

SDA Agents share in 5% of total company revenue, earning based on SDA’s overall success. Only active agents participate in the revenue pool, ensuring that those who actively contribute to SDA’s business expansion are the ones who benefit.

 

5% of all SDA revenue is allocated to the Agent Pool. This includes:

  • B2B revenue from premium SDA-Frame subscriptions, bespoke commissions, and integrations
  • Freemium-to-paid conversions for SDA-Frame
  • Authenticated Digital Collectables (NFT 2.0) revenue, including SDA’s primary sales and resale royalties
  • Licensing agreements and reseller deals with businesses
  • Large-scale art installations and corporate partnerships
  • Earnings are distributed regularly among active agents who contribute to SDA’s commercial and luxury partnerships
  • As SDA scales, the Agent Pool grows, increasing earning potential for engaged agents

This shared revenue model ensures that SDA Agents benefit from long-term business expansion rather than one-time commissions.

Why Join as

an SDA Agent?

  • Earn passive income from SDA-Frame conversions and business partnerships
  • Receive a share of all SDA business revenue, rather than one-time commissions
  • Work with an innovative AI-art brand and expand your influence in high-value industries
  • Create and build relationships with corporate, hospitality, and entertainment clients
  • Operate on your own schedule and build long-term earnings as SDA grows

Who Should Apply?

SDA is looking for business connectors who can help integrate its AI-driven digital art into corporate, hospitality, and luxury spaces. Agents will identify and work with clients to secure custom brand exclusives, high-end licensing deals, and premium digital signage integrations.

 

Ideal candidates include:

  • SDA Members who want to actively expand SDA’s reach by helping place its digital art in more businesses, public spaces, and branded experiences.
  • Business Development & Brand Partnership Experts – Professionals skilled in building relationships with corporate clients, licensing partners, and luxury brands looking for exclusive digital art experiences.
  • Digital Display, Signage & Smart Art Integration Experts – Individuals who work in digital signage, interactive display networks, and corporate AV integrations, positioning SDA’s AI-powered art as a premium upgrade.
  • Luxury, Hospitality, and Commercial Space Specialists – Those in high-end interior design, corporate branding, or hotel guest experience roles who understand how digital art enhances premium environments.
  • Commercial & Luxury Brand Consultants – Experts in brand activation, high-end retail experiences, or NFT-backed digital collectibles who can connect SDA with premium clientele.
  • Art Licensing & Experiential Marketing Strategists – Professionals who work with brands to develop exclusive, custom digital art pieces for product launches, client gifting, or immersive brand storytelling.
  • NFT Enthusiasts – Individuals with a deep understanding of blockchain-backed digital art who can educate collectors and businesses on the long-term value of SDA's artwork.
