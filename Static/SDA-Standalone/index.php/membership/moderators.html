SDA Moderators:

Community Support

& Engagement

SDA Moderators are essential to maintaining a vibrant and well-organized community. They help members navigate SDA’s ecosystem, facilitate discussions, engage with potential collectors and business clients, and provide support for artists and collectors. Moderators also assist with commission inquiries and help ensure smooth interactions across SDA’s platforms.

What Do SDA

Moderators Do?

Moderators play a crucial role in ensuring the SDA community remains engaged, informed, and well-supported. Their responsibilities span across forum management, customer engagement, and assisting members with SDA’s tools and services.

  • Managing Forums & Community Spaces – Oversee discussions on Discord and other community platforms, ensuring productive and respectful conversations.
  • Engaging Potential Collectors & Clients – Answer questions from interested collectors, businesses, and investors, helping them understand SDA’s Authenticated Digital Collectibles (ADCs).
  • Guiding New & Existing Members – Assist artists, collectors, and businesses in navigating SDA’s Discord, forums, and member tools.
  • Commission Ticket Assistance – Help manage commission inquiries, ensuring questions are directed to the appropriate SDA members for resolution.
  • Supporting Artists & Members – Provide assistance with SDA’s internal tools, ensuring artists can publish their work and take advantage of SDA’s ecosystem.

How Moderators

Are Supported

Moderators at SDA benefit from dedicated resources and tools that help streamline their role while contributing to SDA’s growth and success.

  • Access to SDA’s Private Channels – Stay informed with exclusive updates, team discussions, and project insights.
  • Collaboration with SDA Members – Work closely with curators, agents, and administrators to help shape SDA’s community strategies.
  • Early Access to SDA Tools – Gain hands-on experience with SDA’s internal tools, helping test and refine them before wider rollout.
  • Opportunities for Expanded Roles – As we grow SDA, moderators have the chance to take on additional responsibilities and access to other roles.

How Moderator

Earnings Work

Moderators benefit from SDA’s 5% revenue share model, earning passive income from multiple revenue streams, including ADC sales, commissions, and display subscriptions. As SDA’s ecosystem expands, moderators receive growing financial rewards for their ongoing contributions.

  • Authentic Digital Collectibles (ADCs) Sales – A percentage of every ADC sale contributes to the moderator revenue pool.
  • Commissions & Custom Artwork – A portion of SDA’s commission-based work is allocated to moderators for assisting with inquiries and community support.
  • SDA-Frame Premium Subscriptions – Revenue from businesses subscribing to SDA’s curated digital art feeds contributes to the pool.
  • Gallery & Licensing Deals – Any revenue generated from SDA’s curated gallery placements and brand licensing supports moderator earnings.

The more SDA grows, the more moderators benefit, making this a long-term, scalable revenue model tied to SDA’s success.

Why Join as

an SDA Moderator?

  • Earn passive income from SDA’s moderator revenue pool, including ADC sales and commission inquiries.
  • Help shape SDA’s digital art community, supporting both artists and collectors in an innovative ecosystem.
  • Gain exclusive access to SDA’s tools, projects, and community-building initiatives.
  • Expand your participation as SDA grows, with opportunities for additional roles and deeper community engagement.
  • Work on your own schedule while contributing to a fast-growing collective of artists, collectors, and businesses.

Who Should Apply?

SDA is looking for dedicated moderators who can help manage discussions, assist collectors and artists, and support SDA’s growing presence in the digital art space.

  • Community Managers & Forum Moderators – Those experienced in managing online discussions and engaging communities.
  • Collectors & Digital Art Enthusiasts – Individuals passionate about ADCs and eager to help others understand and engage with SDA.
  • Support Specialists – Those skilled in answering questions, routing inquiries, and assisting members with tools and services.
  • Artists & SDA Members – Existing members who want to contribute to SDA’s success while supporting fellow creators.
