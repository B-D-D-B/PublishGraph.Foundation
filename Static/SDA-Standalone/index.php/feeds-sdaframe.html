How SDA-Frame Revenue is Distributed

Supporting artists, members, and the growth of the digital art ecosystem.

🎨 60% – Artists
The majority of SDA-Frame revenue goes directly to artists who contribute artwork to SDA’s digital feeds, ensuring sustainable passive income for creators.
🚀 10% – Partner Mutual Growth Exchange Fund
SDA’s Growth Exchange Fund is a mutual support system with its two core technology partners, who provide infrastructure and platform development. SDA contributes 10% of revenue in return for reciprocal contributions as partners grow.
💼 25% – Support Members
Moderators, Curators, Marketers, and Developers receive a share of revenue, ensuring that those who help sustain SDA’s growth are fairly compensated.
⚖️ 5% – Legal & Core IT Services
Funds allocated to SDA’s legal, financial, and IT infrastructure to keep contracts, royalties, and operational logistics secure and beneficial for members.
