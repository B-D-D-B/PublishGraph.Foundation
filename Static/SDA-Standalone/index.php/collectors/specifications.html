SDA Collector Specifications

SDA’s Authenticated Digital Collectibles (ADCs) set a new standard for high-end digital ownership, delivering ultra-high-resolution artwork, next-generation display compatibility, and seamless integration with both digital and physical products. Designed for collectors, businesses, and investors, ADCs go beyond speculative NFTs by offering tangible, premium-quality assets built for luxury displays, AR experiences, and authenticated product integration.

 

Each ADC is structured on SDA’s NFT 2.0 proposal, providing blockchain authentication and secure provenance while incorporating cutting-edge enhancements for display technology and digital collectability. Whether you’re looking to showcase museum-grade digital art, integrate unique collectibles into premium product lines, or leverage authenticated digital assets, ADCs provide a high-value, future-proof solution.

 

SDA-ADC Categories

SDA ADCs are structured into three distinct groups, providing clarity on how each piece is categorized within the SDA ecosystem.

Main Releases

  • The foundation of SDA’s ADC releases, issued in structured sets or curated runs.
  • Each Main Release ADC belongs to a thematically curated collection.
  • Officially indexed with a collection number, release number, and artist name for provenance.

Commissions

  • Bespoke, collector-requested artworks outside of standard releases or custom mints.
  • Each piece is assigned a commission number and title, integrated into the SDA Commission Collection.
  • Organized by year and treated as an independent run within the SDA system.

Custom Mints or "Alts"

  • Non-release ADCs that did not make it into an official Main Release or is a piece of personal artwork and not part of a project.
  • Typically alternate versions, amusing AI quirks, or exceptional pieces that didn’t meet specific criteria but still stand out as unique and collectible.
  • Minted on demand—collectors can browse eligible Custom Mints from our curated galleries and choose to have them minted.
  • Once minted, the artwork is marked as taken, making it a first-come, first-serve limited edition minting process.



High-End Collector Specifications

Authenticated Digital Collectibles (ADCs) are SDA’s answer to the next generation of digital media ownership, offering premium, high-resolution artwork that is built for luxury digital frames, augmented reality (AR), and authenticated product integration. Improving on conventional NFTs, ADCs focus on display excellence and functional value, making them an ideal choice for collectors and brands that demand more from their digital assets.

 

With SDA’s NFT 2.0 infrastructure, every ADC is backed by secure blockchain authentication, ensuring ownership verification, resale tracking, and exclusive access to SDA’s premium art ecosystem. ADCs are designed for those who appreciate quality, uniqueness, and integration with the future of digital art and media.

Ultra-High-Resolution Artwork

  • 16K resolution scaling (longest side), offering up to 256-megapixel images.
  • Future-proofed for next-generation digital displays and print applications.
  • Dual-NFT System – Collectors receive both full-resolution unwatermarked ADC for personal use and a watermarked version for curated public display.

Advanced Metadata & Provenance

SDA’s three-tier metadata framework ensures every ADC is authentic, trackable, and optimized for display and product integration.

  • Core Metadata – Defines the ADC’s permanent identity, including collection structure, artist attribution, and display settings.
  • Lifecycle Metadata – Tracks ownership changes, valuations, staking, and product redemptions over time.
  • Update Log – Ensures full transparency by recording all metadata modifications with blockchain verification.
