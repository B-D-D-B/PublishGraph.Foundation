SDA Frames: Collections of artwork categorized thematically and distributed to Frame subscribers.

 

*We plan to finalize these core Frames in the second quarter of 2025 as a Collective activity. This includes selecting a name for each Frame from the three options provided as well as adding additional hashtags, if needed.

 

SDA organizes its collection into 12 themed frames, all accessible through the free version of SDA Frame, which updates every five minutes. While we are currently in the concept phase as we complete cataloging our base collection, you can explore different frames easily.

To try them out, click 'Open in New Tab', then drag the tab into view. Each link will reuse the same tab, allowing you to switch between frames seamlessly.

 

You can also switch frames once you have SDA Frame open by clicking on the watermark icon in the bottom left of the frame and switching through there.

1. Humor & Satire

Open | Open in new Tab

🔹Top Name Contender: Jest

🔹Other Options: Wit, Snark

🔹Theme: Playful, clever, and eye-catching—this frame includes humor-driven artwork, witty visual design, and engaging commercial aesthetics.

🔹Description: The Jest Frame highlights art that surprises, delights, and engages. This can include ironic advertisements, surreal product packaging, whimsical greeting cards, and clever social commentary. While political cartoons and satire are part of the mix, this frame is broader—focusing on art that plays with perception, marketing aesthetics, and visual humor.

Example Tags:

#VisualWordplay #IronicAds #WhimsicalIllustrations #WittyDesign #AbsurdistAesthetics #HumorousConceptArt
