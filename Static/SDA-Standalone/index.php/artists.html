Artists:

Relaxed Community that Scales with Your Interests

SDA is a community built by artists—focused on creativity, collaboration, and independence. We welcome all types of digital artists to explore new ideas, take on creative challenges, and build collections within a community. Members can work independently, collaborate on group challenges, or contribute to commercial projects—using their usual workflows or integrating our internally developed tools to reduce AI costs, streamline processes, and simplify publishing across social feeds, display networks, and NFT collections.

 

Inspired by our ANSI art group roots, SDA runs on a merit-based system where members lead the way—no corporate oversight, no outside control. Support roles like moderators, curators and operators help drive the collective forward and share in its rewards.

Where SDA Began: From ANSI Art to Generative Futures

SDA’s roots trace back to the early 1990s, when underground pirate BBS ANSI groups pioneered high-energy ANSI art, ASCII graphics, and experimental digital compositions in the pre-internet era. Just as those early artists pushed the boundaries of limited technology, SDA continues that spirit today—blending generative art, AI tools, and creative collaboration to shape the future of digital art.

 

We like keeping things light and flexible—whether you want to vote on art challenge topics for BlueSky, contribute independent work to a Frame, or publish to our viewer feeds. For those looking to get more involved, SDA offers collaborative opportunities across a range of creative projects, from community challenges to professional commissions. SDA seeks to balance community-driven collaboration with real-world opportunities, helping artists enjoy the creative process while building clear paths to exposure and commercial success.

SDA organizes its creative work into Frames—broad thematic categories that allow artists to explore different genres, styles, and creative directions. Whether you specialize in surreal AI landscapes, sci-fi worldbuilding, or classic fine art influences, there’s a Frame that fits your work.

  • Explore SDA Frames - Find one or more styles within SDA’s broad-themed Frames to organize your work into SDA's art catalog.
  • Contribute to Projects - Discover projects by theme and join to collaborate on the ones that inspire you.
  • Propose New Projects - Suggest and launch projects that align with one or more Frames.

 

Exposure & Royalties

SDA helps artists showcase their work and explore multiple paths to visibility and income. Whether you want to share your art freely, distribute through curated feeds, or sell as NFTs, you’re in control.

  • Show Your Work - Feature your art in personal galleries, SDA-Frame feeds, and community collections.
  • Earn from SDA-Frame - Opt in to premium feeds for greater exposure and revenue opportunities.
  • Sell Directly - Offer artwork through direct-to-print galleries & authentic digital collectable (NFT 2.0) sales.
  • Join SDA Projects - Contribute to curated collections with structured distribution.

Commissions

SDA artist commissions operate through blockchain, ensuring transparency and security. ACD (NFT 2.0) coupons serve as commission "tickets," purchased by individuals, businesses, or SDA itself, with fulfillment managed through smart contracts.

  • Commission Ticket NFTs - SDA sells individual or bundled commission ACD coupons that collectors or businesses can redeem through the SDA ticketing system.
  • Investor Bundles - Ticket commissions issued as part of investor packages.
  • SDA Work Orders - Fulfill contract-based work orders issued by SDA for businesses and commercial partners.

SDA Technology: Create, Publish, and Engage

SDA provides artists with a seamless workflow for AI-assisted creation, refinement, and distribution through its two core applications:

  • SDA-Fusion → A full-featured artist workflow hub for managing, refining, and publishing generative art.
  • SDA-Frame → A curated distribution and engagement platform for showcasing and sharing artwork.

Together, these tools enable artists to produce high-quality AI-assisted work, automate key processes, and publish directly to SDA’s ecosystem.

 

These tools are owned by SDA and built on platforms provided by our partner technology companies. Our members dictate their future direction.

Membership Overview: Easy-to-start options

Being part of SDA is meant to be fun and rewarding. Take what inspires you, leave what doesn’t - we’re here to create, share, and explore new frontiers in art. For some, that means quietly sharing their work in feeds, enjoying the excitement of reaching a wider audience. For others, it’s about seeing their art out in the world - displayed in public spaces, collected as NFTs, or becoming part of carefully curated collections. However you engage, SDA offers a space to create on your own terms.

  • Choose Your Level of Involvement Whether you're here to explore, collaborate, or lead, membership is based on participation, not fees.
  • Find Your Role in the Collective Beyond artists, SDA grows on contributors who amplify, curate, develop, and moderate—support roles also share in dedicated royalty pools.
