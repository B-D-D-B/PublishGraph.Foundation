## Outcomes and bounded cancellation

Only successful completion of SMTP DATA with final positive acknowledgement and fixed recipient in Nodemailer's accepted result yields `'accepted'` and HTTP202. RCPT acceptance alone cannot yield success. This is provider acceptance for sending, never inbox proof; later bounces remain possible. Explicit SMTP negative replies (AUTH, MAIL FROM, RCPT TO or final DATA refusal) and proven TLS verification failure yield a resolved nonaccepted result and existing HTTP503. Transport timeout/disconnect/abort or other uncertain error after initiation throws a sanitized error and maps to existing HTTP504 `send_outcome_unknown`; conservative unknown is acceptable where pre-DATA refusal cannot be proven. No error/timeout refunds the consumed daily reservation.

The existing outer 10-second `bounded` AbortSignal must terminate the owned in-flight connection, not merely stop awaiting it. Use Nodemailer's supported socket hook or equivalent bounded ownership: obtain/retain the socket as soon as created (including DNS/connect/TLS pending), destroy it and close the owned transporter on abort/finally, guard every asynchronous continuation against an already-aborted operation, and never pass a late socket to SMTP after cancellation. A pre-aborted signal causes no connection; abort while DNS/connect is pending cannot later authenticate or submit DATA. Remove listeners/timers on settlement. Configure finite connection/greeting/socket timeouts no longer than the outer budget; never reuse aborted transports or retry. Preserve the handler's 10-second contract, 50/day conditional reservation behavior, public status shapes and unknown-outcome warning. No exactly-once guarantee is introduced.
